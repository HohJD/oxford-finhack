About

One day to build a working FinTech product, for business school students, finance professionals, entrepreneurs and engineers.

Teams tackle real challenges across the financial industry and build practical solutions with Devin. Whether you come from finance, technology or just have an idea worth exploring, you will work across disciplines and leave with something that runs.

FAQ

Do I need coding experience?

No. Participants build using Devin, and teams mix finance, business and technical backgrounds.

Can I join without a team?

Yes. Individual applicants can form teams on the day.

My team is already formed. Do we register together?

No, every participant must register individually.

Is food provided?

Yes, refreshments and meals are provided throughout the day.

What can we win?

Each member of the first-place team receives $500 in Devin credits. Each member of the second- and third-place teams receives $200 in Devin credits.
